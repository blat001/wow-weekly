# WoW Weekly Tracker

Tick any combination of weekly activities for each character in the
**Characters** settings tab: Balanced, Delves and Hero gear, Mythic+ Vault,
Raid Vault, and Profession knowledge. Balanced includes other eligible
weeklies; untick it to focus on selected activities. Choose one, two, or three
Vault slots as the target for each selected gear row. Balanced keeps a full
raid target unless Raid is explicitly selected. “Weekly targets met” means
you have done enough; it is distinct from completing every Vault slot.
Selections survive weekly resets. Existing single goals migrate automatically.

The separate **Bonus-roll option** checkbox counts choices across
World, Dungeon, and Raid together, at any difficulty. For example, two World
slots plus one Dungeon slot meet the target. It suggests the enabled row with
the shortest estimated time to its next slot after selected activity
targets are met. Three choices unlock the
option to take a bonus-roll token instead of gear from the next Vault.
Gear-focused slot targets remain specific to each chosen row. The checkbox
can accompany any combination, or be used alone with all activities unticked.
An old bonus-roll-only goal migrates to the checkbox with no activity goals.

**Play Next** ranks all tracked characters independently of the status columns.
Confirmed raids starting within two hours come first, followed by estimated time
to the next reward in a selected goal. Similar effort is ordered by potential
gear benefit (reward item level versus average equipped item level), then your
activity preferences. Completed goals go last. The ranking has its own order
across pages, so its first page can recommend an alt on another status page.

Times are planning estimates, not measured clear times: about 20 minutes per
Delve, 40 per Mythic+ run plus 15 for setup, and 20 per raid boss plus 45 for
forming a group unless a confirmed raid starts soon. Unknown progress or quest
duration is shown without a time estimate and follows known estimates. Vault
goals use the next slot, not overall percentage complete or the full weekly cap.
Raid reward labels name gear tracks, not raid difficulty.
World-boss and LFR recommendations require a known average item level of 279
or lower. Their status and earned Vault slots remain visible at higher item
levels; Normal and higher raid recommendations remain eligible.

**See every weekly chore for all your characters at a glance, and know exactly who to log in next.**

WoW Weekly Tracker shows the weekly tasks you actually care about across your main and all your alts in one clean grid. It is focused and lightweight, built for delves, keyshards, professions and the weekly objectives, without the clutter of doing everything at once.

## Features

- **All characters in one grid.** Open the minimap or Titan button to see every tracked character side by side. The character you are on always appears first.
- **Play Next.** A ranked list above the status table suggests who to play for the next selected reward, with rough remaining time and time-sensitive raid opportunities first.
- **Gear-aware.** Delves and Coiled Isle automatically grey out for characters already in Mythic-level gear, since they no longer upgrade you. M+ and raid never get hidden.
- **Organized by category** with clear sections and colored status, so you can read it at a glance.
- **Pick your characters and rows.** Tracking, Game Plan, and Characters tabs let you choose what appears and set weekly goals. Goal explanations appear on hover.

## What it tracks

- **Delves**: Bounty Map (Not Looted / In Bags / Used), Coffer Keys, Coffer Key Shards, and the weekly Gilded Stash (up to 4)
- **Coiled Isle**: weekly completion progress
- **Weekly Quest**: the rotating weekly meta objective and the Timewalking event weekly
- **Prey**: Normal, Hard and Nightmare progress
- **Professions**: weekly knowledge quest and treatise for both professions, Darkmoon Faire quests while the monthly Faire is open, plus "ready to craft" status. Darkmoon completion survives weekly resets.
- **Great Vault**: World, Dungeon, Raid and your Keystone

The named raid section counts bosses in that raid only. The Great Vault raid
row includes all qualifying raid and lair kills. Its labels (such as `[Hero]`)
describe the reward's gear upgrade track, not the difficulty you completed.

## How to use

1. Install and log in.
2. Click the minimap button (or the Titan Panel plugin) to open the grid.
3. Click the grid footer to open options and choose which characters and categories to show.
4. Log into each character once so it appears in the grid. Everything resets automatically each week.

Made for players who just want their weekly checklist, done.
